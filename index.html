MORTUUS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-26

This policy explains what data MORTUUS (the "Game") collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.
The Game is published by Hamuno ("we", "us"). Contact: hamunoobusiness@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

When you play the Game (full game or demo), it sends us the following:

  • Steam identity: your Steam ID and Steam display name. We do not receive your Steam password, email address, payment details or friends list.
  • Gameplay data: things like the rounds you reach, kills, deaths, guns and upgrades picked, maps played, how long sessions last, and where on a map players get stuck.
  • Settings: your in-game settings and rebound keys, so we can see which defaults work.
  • Device and performance data: operating system, graphics card and driver, graphics API, CPU, memory, screen size, frame rate and stutter measurements.
  • Crash and error data: error codes and the last part of the game's log when a session ends unexpectedly.
  • Approximate location: your country, worked out from your connection. Your IP address is used only to work out your country and to limit abuse. It is not kept with your gameplay data.
  • Co-op and multiplayer: other players in your session can see your Steam name and in-game actions. Text chat messages are shared only with players in your session. Voice chat is shared live with nearby players in your session and is not recorded or stored by us.
  • Workshop and map creator: maps you publish to the Steam Workshop are public through Steam.

2. Anti-cheat and tamper checks

The Game checks for software that modifies it (such as mod loaders, cracked Steam emulators or altered game libraries). If it finds something, it may send a report to us containing your Steam ID, the names of the files found and basic system information, and then close the Game. These checks look only at the Game's own folder and the Game's own process, not your personal files.

The Game's code and data are encrypted and obfuscated to protect it from piracy and cheating. This is standard protection and does not affect your files or your computer.

Tampering with the Game, cheating or abusing other players can lead to a ban, as set out in our End User License Agreement: https://hamunoo.github.io/mortuus-eula/

3. Why we use it

  • To find and fix bugs, crashes and performance problems.
  • To balance the Game's difficulty, prices and rewards.
  • To detect cheating and piracy and keep multiplayer fair.
  • To keep the Game working across different computers.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising. We do not share it with third parties except service providers who host our systems for us.

4. Legal basis (EU/UK players)

Where GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on our legitimate interest in running, securing and improving the Game, and on performance of the contract when you play it. You can object at any time (section 6).

5. How long we keep it

We keep gameplay and performance data for up to 24 months, and anti-cheat reports for up to 24 months or as long as needed to enforce a ban. Then it is deleted or anonymized.

6. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about your Steam ID, correct it, delete it, or stop collecting it. Email hamunoobusiness@gmail.com from the address you use for your Steam account, and give your Steam ID. We will answer within 30 days. If you are in the EU or UK you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections to servers we control. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit access to the people who need it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for big changes, announce it on the Steam page.
